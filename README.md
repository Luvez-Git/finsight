# \# FinSight

# 

# \### Turning financial data into actionable intelligence.

# 

# FinSight is a banking customer intelligence platform designed to help financial institutions understand customer behavior and turn that information into actionable business decisions.

# 

# The platform brings together customer analytics, churn intelligence, customer value analysis, and cross-sell opportunities in a single dashboard.

# 

# \## Overview

# 

# Banks collect large amounts of customer and transaction data, but raw data alone does not tell decision-makers what action to take.

# 

# FinSight addresses this by transforming customer data into clear business intelligence around three key questions:

# 

# \*\*Who is at risk?\*\*  

# Identify customers who may be at risk of leaving the bank.

# 

# \*\*Who matters most?\*\*  

# Identify high-value customers and understand which customer relationships are most valuable.

# 

# \*\*What should the bank do next?\*\*  

# Identify potential cross-sell opportunities and recommend relevant financial products based on customer characteristics and behavior.

# 

# \## Core Features

# 

# \### Churn Intelligence

# 

# FinSight analyzes customer activity and behavioral indicators to classify customers into different levels of churn risk:

# 

# \- Low Risk

# \- Medium Risk

# \- High Risk

# 

# This allows banking teams to identify customers who may require retention attention before they become inactive or leave.

# 

# \### Customer Value

# 

# The Customer Value module helps identify customers who represent stronger business value to the bank.

# 

# It considers factors such as:

# 

# \- Average balance

# \- Monthly income

# \- Transaction activity

# \- Number of products

# \- Customer value score

# \- Account characteristics

# 

# Customers can then be grouped into value segments for easier analysis.

# 

# \### Cross-Sell Intelligence

# 

# FinSight identifies customers who may have opportunities for additional financial products.

# 

# Instead of treating every customer the same, the platform uses customer information and behavioral indicators to identify potential opportunities and provide recommended products and actions.

# 

# \### Customer Intelligence

# 

# The Customers section provides a searchable view of individual customer records, allowing analysts to investigate customer-level insights rather than relying only on aggregate statistics.

# 

# \### Analytics Dashboard

# 

# The main dashboard provides an overview of the most important customer intelligence metrics, including:

# 

# \- Total customers

# \- High-risk customers

# \- High-value customers

# \- Cross-sell opportunities

# \- Churn risk distribution

# \- Priority actions

# 

# \### CSV Export

# 

# Analysts can export customer intelligence data to CSV for further analysis or use by other banking teams such as customer retention, sales, and relationship management.

# \## Why FinSight?

# 

# FinSight is built around a simple principle:

# 

# > \*\*Data becomes valuable when it leads to a decision.\*\*

# 

# Rather than presenting banking data as raw tables and statistics, FinSight organizes customer intelligence around practical business questions.

# 

# This helps banking teams move from simply observing customer data to identifying where attention and action may be needed.

# 

# \## Project Purpose

# 

# FinSight aims to help banks turn customer and transaction data into actionable decisions—improving customer retention, identifying high-value customer relationships, and making product recommendations more targeted and explainable.

#
